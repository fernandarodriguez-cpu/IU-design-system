# Khor Design System - Credits & Acknowledgments

Khor Design System (KDS) is an independent, enterprise-grade UI architecture. 

However, we believe in the open-source community and the giants whose shoulders we stand upon. We would like to formally acknowledge the following projects which served as structural and architectural inspiration during the early phases of KDS:

- **Radix UI**: For their unstyled, accessible components which inspired our approach to headless primitives and ARIA attributes.
- **Ant Design**: For their robust, enterprise-ready component matrix and component parity definitions.
- **Shadcn UI**: For popularizing the "copy-paste" ownership model and utility-class driven architectures.

While KDS is now a fully autonomous system with its own design tokens, responsive logic, and Figma MCP synchronization engines, we extend our gratitude to these communities.
